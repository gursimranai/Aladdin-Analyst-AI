# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Aladdin Analyst AI project a welcoming, respectful, and constructive experience for everyone.

We expect contributors, maintainers, users, and community members to interact professionally and respectfully.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

* Being respectful and constructive.
* Welcoming different perspectives.
* Giving and receiving constructive feedback.
* Focusing discussions on ideas and technical problems.
* Respecting different levels of experience.
* Helping others learn.
* Taking responsibility for mistakes.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment or discrimination.
* Personal attacks.
* Threats or intimidation.
* Deliberate disruption of discussions.
* Publishing private information without permission.
* Trolling or insulting behavior.
* Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source community.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers through the project's designated private communication channel.

For security vulnerabilities, do not use public issues. Follow the process described in `SECURITY.md`.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code of conduct.

Maintainers may take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior, including warnings, moderation, temporary restrictions, or removal from project participation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and interactions where participants are representing or participating in the Aladdin Analyst AI community.
